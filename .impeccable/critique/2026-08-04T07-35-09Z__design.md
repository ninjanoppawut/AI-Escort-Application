## Design Specificity Verdict

**Grounded in this product — with a concept that is asserted but never rendered.**

**LLM assessment.** A meaningful set of controls here could not be lifted into another product. The trait-verification row (S-19) — four states with the AI value struck through and a correction field opening in place — is a purpose-built epistemic instrument, not a form widget. The AI-provisional treatment (dashed border + mono `AI · ชั่วคราว` tab) is applied without a single exception across S-17, S-18, S-19, S-26, T-12 mobile and T-12 desktop, encoding the product's central claim in geometry rather than copy. S-14 draws the GPS accuracy circle at true scale and states in the UI that the pin uses start-of-record position. S-21 renders same-species and same-specimen as two differently-coloured chips with a distance readout — a distinction almost every other product would collapse into one warning.

Where it goes category-interchangeable: the container vocabulary is stock (12px card, 1px border, 999px chip, 40/90 sheet, 5-slot nav) — defensible in Operate mode. The real problem is **structural sameness**: all 33 hi-fi frames are one skeleton — status bar → 56px header → 16px body → white action block. S-22 (you succeeded) and S-23 (your teacher sent your work back) are the same chassis with different fills. Composition never responds to stakes.

**The unbuilt concept.** The brief names "สมุดบันทึกภาคสนาม" — field notebook — and delivers no notebook. No paper grain, no ruled baseline, no marginal annotation, no botanical-plate convention. What ships is competent green-tinted SaaS. S-26/T-16 are literally specimen records already carrying collector, date, locality and determiner — the exact four fields of a herbarium label — rendered as a generic key-value grid. Biggest missed opportunity for character in the set, and it costs nothing in scanability.

**Second missed opportunity.** `statusTokens` assigns a distinct *shape* per observation status, applies it to badges and chips, then abandons it on the map — where every pin is the same teardrop separated only by fill. The system's most product-specific visual idea is switched off on the screen it was invented for.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI detector: exit 2, **7 findings** across 4 of 6 files, all `warning`/`slop` — `side-tab` ×5, `em-dash-overuse` ×2. Assessment B opened every cited line and classified them: **6 of 7 are the deliverable documents' own annotation chrome** (amber left-rules marking designer notes in Wireframes/Prototype, green left-rules on the Brief's constraint grid). Exactly one — `Student Hi-Fi:373` — sits in product UI.

The browser detector, run in-page after verified script injection, found far more: **Student Hi-Fi 71, Teacher Hi-Fi 77, Prototype 16**. The dominant families are `undersized-ui-text` (39 / 33 / 9), `low-contrast` (18 / 15 / 4) and `tiny-text` (7 / 24 / 1). B tested each node for containment in a ~390px phone frame: **39/39 undersized-text and 17/18 low-contrast hits on Student Hi-Fi are product UI, not chrome.**

This is where the two assessments converge hardest. A reached "field mode violates its own field rules" by reading the brief's 15px/56px/7:1 requirements against the drawn screens. B reached the same conclusion by measuring rendered nodes in a browser, with no knowledge of the brief. Worst measured contrast — **2.0:1** on `＋ สร้างกลุ่มของฉัน` — is the disabled Create Group control the brief specifically requires to stay legible with a visible reason rather than vanish.

**False positives**, per B: the four document-chrome `side-tab` hits (a deliberate, consistently applied spec-annotation convention); both `em-dash-overuse` hits (the rule is calibrated for English prose at ~1 dash per 500 chars — Thai has no inter-word spaces, which inflates density, and `·`/`—` are Thai field separators, not rhetorical dashes); and 4 of 5 `cramped-padding` hits (the buttons are `display:grid` + `place-items:center` with explicit 48–56px heights — padding reads 0 because grid does the centring).

**Visual overlays.** Injection genuinely succeeded — mutation was preflighted by setting `document.title` and executing an appended `<script>`, then confirmed by 141 overlay DOM elements on Student Hi-Fi. All three pages rendered fully with no unpopulated `{{ }}` placeholders. Both servers are stopped and verified down; no file in `design/` was created or modified.

## Overall Impression

The thinking is better than the drawing. The information architecture, the status system, the three-layer evidence model and the Thai copy are all stronger than what most products ship — and then the hi-fi screens quietly violate the measurements the brief chose *because of the usage scene*. A student squinting at `±6 ม.` in direct sun, at 11.5px and 6.5:1, cannot tell whether their pin is trustworthy — which is the entire premise of the observation record.

The single biggest opportunity: **enforce the brief's own field rules in the hi-fi.** Not new design work — the correct values are already written down. Two independent assessments, one semantic and one instrumental, converged on the same failure.

## What's Working

**1. The last-slot-race recovery (S-05) treats a race condition as a redirect, not an error.** Blue informational styling rather than red, a causal explanation, a live-freshness strip (`อัปเดตล่าสุดเมื่อ 2 วินาทีที่แล้ว`), and two joinable alternatives with seat counts in the same viewport. Most products ship this as a red toast reading `GROUP_LIMIT_REACHED` and strand the student. It works because the design decided *before* styling that losing a slot is not the student's fault, then let every visual choice follow.

**2. The three-layer evidence model is applied without exception across five files.** Numbered `① นักเรียนสรุปว่า` (solid green) / `② AI · ชั่วคราว` (dashed grey, mono tag) / `③ การตรวจลักษณะของนักเรียน`, identical on T-12 mobile, T-12 desktop and S-26, mirrored in the student flow. The AI never borrows the teacher's verified green; the dashed frame is never reused for anything else. A semantic rule enforced as a hard constraint, so a teacher scanning at speed can identify the provenance of any claim without reading a word.

**3. The status token system specifies four redundant channels per state** — colour, shape, glyph and Thai text — plus separate patterns for stale location and clusters, backed by 25 rows of real Thai copy for loading/empty/offline/conflict/permission states. More complete than most shipped design systems. Which is exactly why the one place it isn't applied is so conspicuous.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The scientific-name requirement contradicts itself across deliverables — and the screen it lives on has no hi-fi

**What.** Wireframes S-20 marks `ชื่อวิทยาศาสตร์ *` required, annotated "ชื่อวิทยาศาสตร์ว่างหรือใส่คำว่า 'ไม่ทราบ' = ส่งไม่ได้ (D-013)". The Brief's flow S8 agrees. **Forms** — the artifact validation gets built from — declares the same field `opt: true` with "ไม่ระบุก็ส่งได้ แต่ครูอาจขอให้เพิ่มภายหลัง". Meanwhile S-20 is marked `hifi: ★` in the inventory but **no S-20 frame exists**; it appears only as a 35%-opacity backdrop behind the S-21 sheet.

**Why it matters.** D-013 is a locked product rule. The implementation agent must invent behaviour on a non-negotiable, and the screen where it lives is the one screen with no visual spec. Whichever way it resolves, the error copy does not exist.

**Fix.** Pick required, per D-013. Build the S-20 hi-fi at 390×844 including the trait recap card the wireframe already has, the inline error, and the rejection path for a literal `ไม่ทราบ`. Set Forms S-20 to `req: true` with that error string. Drop the invented `ความมั่นใจของฉัน` radio or promote it to a product decision.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P0] Completed-map markers are colour-only — on the product's showcase screen

**What.** In S-25, T-15 and S-12 every plant pin is the same droplet, separated only by fill: `#15803D` verified, `#A15C07` awaiting review, `#B3261E` revision required. `statusTokens` already assigns each a distinct shape, and the S-25 filter chips already carry glyphs (`✓ ↑ !`). The vocabulary exists; it never reaches the pins.

**Why it matters.** Violates the brief's own non-negotiable ("marker states need icon, shape, label, or pattern in addition to colour"). Green/amber/red is the hardest triad for deuteranopia and protanopia, on the screen both roles use to read the outcome of the entire activity. The list-view toggle is the stated fallback, but it discards the spatial information the map exists to convey.

**Fix.** Put the existing `statusTokens` glyph inside each pin head at 12–14px white on fill, and vary head shape per the token's `radius`. Update legends to show shape+glyph, not just a colour swatch. Also document the student's-own-pin treatment — S-25 distinguishes one pin with a 3px border that appears in no legend.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P1] Field mode violates its own field rules — confirmed independently by both assessments

**What.** The brief specifies for field mode: body 16px, primary 56px, contrast ≥7:1, 15px type minimum. Measured in the hi-fi: S-12 status chips (GPS accuracy, sync, timer — the safety-critical readouts) at **11.5px**, white on `#1F6B47` ≈ 6.5:1. Field nav labels **10.5px**. S-19 trait buttons **46px**, with the fourth option wrapping to two lines at 12px. T-09 queue arrows **36px** — below the universal 44px floor. Secondary text `#7C8B83` on white ≈ **3.6:1**, failing AA at 11–12.5px.

The browser detector, with no knowledge of the brief, independently returned 39 `undersized-ui-text` and 17 `low-contrast` findings inside product UI on this file alone — worst case **2.0:1** on the disabled Create Group control.

**Why it matters.** These are not aesthetic misses. They are the exact measurements the design chose because of the usage scene, and they are the readouts a student uses to decide whether their pin is trustworthy.

**Fix.** Field-mode chip text to 13px minimum, chip background to `#14472F` (white ≈ 9.6:1). Field nav labels to 12px. Trait buttons to 56px; either shorten `มองไม่เห็น` to `ไม่เห็น` or stack the four options 2×2 so no label wraps. T-09 arrows to 44px. Replace `#7C8B83` with `#5E6D64` (≈5.1:1) as the secondary text token, never below 12px.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P1] T-12's mobile action bar carries five outcomes and an unlabelled destructive glyph

**What.** `✓ รับรอง` / `✎ แก้แล้วรับรอง` / `↩ ขอให้แก้ไข` / `? ตรวจไม่ได้` / `✕` — five terminal decisions, the fifth (reject) a bare 56px glyph with no text. The desktop panel for the same task correctly shows 3 + `⋯`, so mobile is *denser* than desktop, inverting the responsive logic. Violates the brief's own rule: "ห้ามใช้ไอคอนลอยเดี่ยวเป็นปุ่มสำคัญโดยไม่มีป้ายชื่อ".

**Why it matters.** Rejection is the most consequential decision a teacher makes about a student's work, and it is the least labelled control on the screen — distinguishable from `? ตรวจไม่ได้` only by glyph. A mis-tap costs a student their submission. T-10's `⏸` and `⋯` share the defect.

**Fix.** Mirror desktop on mobile: three labelled actions plus a labelled `ตัวเลือกอื่น` opening a sheet with `? ตรวจสอบไม่ได้` and `✕ ปฏิเสธ (ต้องระบุเหตุผล)` as full-width labelled rows. Label `⏸` as `⏸ พัก`. Add impact-summary confirmations to `จบกลุ่มนี้` and `เปิดรอบสำรวจ`.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] The GPS accuracy rule and the GPS screen disagree — at the exact accuracy drawn

**What.** Forms rule for S-14: "ความแม่นยำแย่กว่า ±20 ม. → ปุ่มหลักเปลี่ยนเป็น 'บันทึกแบบติดธง'". The hi-fi is drawn at **±38 ม.** — twice past threshold — with the green primary reading `ใช้ตำแหน่งนี้ แล้วถ่ายภาพ`, and the flagged-save demoted to a 12.5px tertiary link under a *different name*, `บันทึกแบบแจ้งครู`.

**Why it matters.** The hi-fi is what gets built. Students pin plants at ±38m behind a full-strength green CTA, and the teacher map — whose entire value is marker-equals-plant — degrades silently. The naming split means neither term is recognised.

**Fix.** Redraw S-14 at ±38m showing the swap. Standardise one term across Brief, Forms, Prototype and hi-fi. *(Note: a separate session decision moved this rule to warn-only, flagging on no-fix rather than poor accuracy. Reconcile against that before redrawing.)*

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Amber status token reused as a non-status cue

**What.** `Student Hi-Fi:373` — the `กลุ่มมะขามป้อม (กลุ่มของคุณ)` row in the S-11 queue carries `border-left: 3px solid #A15C07`. This is the sole CLI detector finding inside product UI, and sibling rows have no such border.

**Why it matters.** `#A15C07` is the `submitted · รอตรวจ` observation token. Reusing it to mean "this is your group, waiting in queue" breaks the one-meaning-per-colour discipline the brief sets. A student who has learned amber = "my observation awaits the teacher" meets amber meaning something unrelated. The row already signals identity three ways — ringed numeral, bold name, `(กลุ่มของคุณ)` label — so the border is redundant *and* wrong.

**Fix.** Drop it, or move to `#1F6B47` (identity/action, not status).

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] The Prototype deliverable overflows horizontally at its own target width

**What.** Measured in-browser: `scrollWidth` 1800 vs `clientWidth` 1176 — **624px of page-level horizontal overflow** from a 1760px fixed three-pane container. The right-hand spec panel is clipped at a 1440px window, producing a page-level horizontal scrollbar.

**Why it matters.** Deliverable-level, not product-level — but the Prototype is the artifact stakeholders click, and the clipped pane is the one carrying business rules and form specs.

**Fix.** Make the three-pane shell responsive below 1800px, or collapse the spec panel to a toggle at narrower widths.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (distracted mobile, one thumb, interrupted, slow connection)**
- S-19's trait row is a left-to-right thumb crossing, eleven times. Four 78×46px targets span the full 390px; the most common answer `✓ ตรง` is furthest from a right thumb. The repeated decision sits in the upper two-thirds, outside the bottom-25% zone the design reserves for primary actions. Casey fat-fingers `✕ ไม่ตรง`, which opens a required text field.
- S-14's `✕` sits top-left in a 4-step wizard with no confirmation. One stretch discards a started observation including its GPS fix.
- S-12's `ออก` is a 12px text button 8px from the status chips. Exiting an active field session is one tap on a tiny target.
- Nothing on S-19 or S-20 tells Casey the draft is saved — the rule says autosave fires on every tap; only S-17 says so in the UI.
- The batch retry on S-16 is a 12px `ลองเลย` in a 9px-padded banner: the recovery affordance for a failed upload is the smallest target on screen.

**Sam (screen reader, keyboard-only, low vision, 4.5:1, no colour-only meaning)**
- Colour-only map pins across S-25, T-15, S-12, T-10 and desktop. Sam cannot distinguish `รับรอง` from `ต้องแก้ไข` on the results map.
- `#7C8B83` at ~3.6:1 is the secondary text token — every timestamp, seat count, image count, expiry. Fails AA at 11–13px. Detector independently returned 17 product-UI `low-contrast` hits on Student Hi-Fi alone.
- Disabled primaries at ~2.0:1 (`＋ สร้างกลุ่มของฉัน`, `เข้าโหมดสนาม`). Technically WCAG-exempt — but here the control *is* the message, and the brief explicitly requires it stay visible rather than vanish. At 2:1 in sunlight it has vanished.
- Six unlabelled icon controls: T-12 `✕` (reject), T-10 `⏸` and `⋯`, T-11 `⇅`, S-26 `⤴`, S-12 `◎ ☰ ◫`. The component inventory's a11y column assigns aria-labels to none of them.
- Emoji navigation (`🏠 👥 🌿 📋 🔔`) in both bottom navs. VoiceOver/TalkBack announces "house with garden" before "หน้าแรก" on every tab, the emoji render in each OEM's own colour font, and it contradicts the specified 1.75px outline icon system outright.
- No focus specification exists in any of the six files — no focus-visible token, no tab order, no skip link, no shortcuts. The brief requires keyboard navigation for teacher desktop; T-12 desktop asks a teacher to process 7 items sequentially entirely by mouse.
- Credit where due: status chips consistently pair colour + glyph + text; `CapacityMeter` specifies `aria-label "3 จาก 5 คน ขั้นต่ำ 3"`; `Stepper` specifies "ขั้นที่ 2 จาก 4" as text; `TraitRow` is specified as a radiogroup. The component-level thinking is sound — the hi-fi application is where it breaks.

**Jordan (first-timer, reads everything, literal, abandons rather than guess)**
- `? ไม่แน่ใจ` vs `◌ มองไม่เห็น` are indistinguishable to a literal reader — "I'm not sure" and "I can't see it" overlap heavily for a 14-year-old looking at a leaf margin. No on-screen help explains the distinction. Jordan stalls on the first trait.
- S-05's `ขอให้หัวหน้ากลุ่มเชิญฉัน` states no consequence: who receives it, how long, what if nobody responds. No pending state exists in any deliverable. Jordan taps it, nothing changes, concludes the app is broken.

Jordan reads "fairly confident" and takes the green button as endorsement. The corrective banner is above, blue, 13px; the endorsement is below, brand green, 15.5px bold.
- Two names for one feature (`บันทึกแบบแจ้งครู` / `บันทึกแบบติดธง`). Taking labels literally, Jordan will not connect them.
- S-21 exists in two mutually exclusive versions — the hi-fi sheet (read, then acknowledge) and the Forms spec (a **required** 3-option radio first). If the Forms version ships, the one screen designed to reassure now blocks.

## Questions to Consider

1. The evidence note is the thing the teacher grades — and it is the last field on the hardest screen, typed with one thumb in the sun. What if it were voice-to-text at the moment of observation, or assembled from the corrections the student already made in S-19, with the student editing rather than composing?
2. S-26 and T-16 are herbarium specimen records dressed as CRM detail pages. What would they look like designed as a specimen label — collector, date, locality, determiner — in conventions a botanist would recognise? Is there a version a student would screenshot and send to a parent?
3. Trait verification costs 44 taps minimum per plant. If the pedagogical goal is "compare AI claims against the living plant," is per-trait four-way classification the cheapest instrument that achieves it — or would "which three of these AI claims are wrong?" produce more genuine looking and less mechanical tapping?
4. S-19's four options collapse two different kinds of uncertainty — `ไม่แน่ใจ` is epistemic, `มองไม่เห็น` is observational. That distinction is scientifically meaningful and taught nowhere. Should the first trait a student ever verifies carry an inline explanation, once?
5. The teacher verifies every observation manually, one at a time, five outcomes. With 32 students × ~4 plants that is ~128 sequential five-way decisions. What is the design for a teacher on decision #90?
6. A student who loses the last slot and is never invited has no path they control. Should the invite request become a real object with a pending state and teacher escalation — or should the teacher simply be blocked from opening a session while `3 คนยังไม่มีกลุ่ม` stands? T-01 already surfaces that count.
7. The "field notebook" concept exists only in the brief's prose. If it is real, which one screen should look unmistakably like a notebook — and if the answer is "none, because Operate outranks expression," should the concept be renamed to what the design actually is?
